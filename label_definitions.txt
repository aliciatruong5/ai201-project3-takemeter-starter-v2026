info: shares checkable facts (a stat, time, date, result, or rule) and the post is mainly about delivering them; the writer is informing, not asking or emoting
experience: the writer describes something that happened to them personally (a run, a race, an injury, a milestone) and how it felt; the focus is their own story or feelings, not facts for others or a request for help
question: the main purpose of the post is to ask for an answer, advice, or opinion from other readers; it has a question or an explicit request, even if some context or facts come first
